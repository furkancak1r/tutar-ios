### Description

Tutar is a free iPhone and iPad app for simple income and expense tracking, budget planning, and credit card installment management. Your financial data stays on your device and can optionally sync through your private iCloud account.

What you can do:
• Add income and expenses in seconds with the automatic-cents keypad.
• Track monthly budgets, category budgets, and spending analysis.
• Configure one-time, repeating, or installment entries from one schedule menu.
• Preserve installment totals to the cent and see future payments only in their due month.
• Use Turkish or English with every ISO currency.
• Import Dime/Tutar CSV and export transaction CSV or a complete JSON backup.
• Protect the app with optional Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode.
• Use the widget and local reminders for quick access.
• Track gold, silver, and currency savings in the currency selected in Settings using automatic cross rates or a manual unit price.

Tutar is completely free. It contains no ads, analytics, trackers, accounts, subscriptions, purchases, donations, or paywalls.
